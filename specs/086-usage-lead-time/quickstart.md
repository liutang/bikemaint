# Quickstart: 086 Usage Lead Time

1. **Settings:** the Lead time card shows 7 / 20 / 2 / 2. Change Miles to 25; it reads "Saved", and the summary updates.
2. **A chain on a bike** with 18 mi left and a date a month away is on the attention board as due soon, with the ring reading "18 mi", and one notification is recorded.
3. **The same chain in storage** isn't due soon.
4. **Mute notifications:** nothing is recorded.
5. **Set Miles to 0:** the chain falls back to the date rule.
6. **Tests:** run `.venv/bin/python -m pytest -q`.
