# Quickstart: 073 Builder Drag and Collapse

Use a bike with a wheelset holding two wheels, each wheel with a tire, plus a few top-level parts. Use a desktop browser.

1. **Reorder (US1)**:
   1. Drag Stem over the top of Chain. The gap opens above Chain and reads "on the bike". Drop it.
   2. Stem now saves before Chain.
2. **After the last part (US1-3)**:
   1. Drag a part over the bottom of the last tire on Front wheel. The gap is at the tire's level and reads "on Front wheel".
   2. Drop it. The part is now last on Front wheel.
3. **Inside (US1-4/5)**:
   1. Over the middle of a tire, the gap is indented under the tire and the tire is outlined.
   2. Over a wheel whose tires are showing, the gap becomes the wheel's first sub-part.
4. **Smoothness (US1-6/10)**: move slowly down the whole tree. Rows slide, and the gap never flickers between two spots.
5. **Cancel (US1-12)**: press Escape mid-drag. The tree is exactly as before.
6. **Collapse (US2)**:
   1. Click Front wheel's chevron. The tires slide away and the row reads "2 parts".
   2. Reload the page, or move another part. Front wheel is still collapsed.
   3. Click the chevron again to open it.
7. **Collapsed drag (US3)**:
   1. Hover the middle of a collapsed Front wheel. The gap shows alone under it.
   2. Hold for under a second. The wheel opens and the gap becomes its first sub-part.
   3. Drop. The wheel stays open.
8. **Without scripts**: the tree is fully open, and the Garage, Trash and Add to… forms work.
